ROOM-LENS v1.1

Live ASCII room mapper. Renderer lifted from SuperInstance/chiaroscuro (Mirror door + Sobel shape layer). Adaptive fidelity: still = finer grid, moving = faster/coarser. Walkabout banks ASCII frames + pose stamps + microphone audio on one clock for the splat lane. MEASURE ROOM records a swept-sine for RT60. Bundles download — or upload straight to the fleet.
